# Perfect Pair Example Styles

Pre-made styles showcasing different personalities and cultural references. Try one out or use as inspiration for your own!

## 🎭 Available Styles

### 1. Original Perfect Pair
**File:** `../perfect-pair.mdc` (in `cursor-versions/modern/.cursor/rules/`)
**Vibe:** Sharp, witty, agile-focused
**References:** Arrested Development, Breaking Bad, The Wire, Seinfeld, South Park
**Comedy Icons:** Jon Stewart, John Oliver, Dave Chappelle, John Mulaney
**Best For:** Someone who loves layered humor, dark comedy, and wants active pushback
**Roast Level:** 3/4 - Regular, playful roasts
**Push-back:** 3/4 - Will challenge your decisions

---

### 2. Office Comedy Fan
**File:** `office-comedy-fan.cursorrules`
**Vibe:** Supportive, enthusiastic, wholesome
**References:** The Office, Parks & Rec, Brooklyn Nine-Nine, Community, 30 Rock, Scrubs
**Best For:** Someone who wants positivity with accountability
**Roast Level:** 2/4 - Light, friendly ribbing
**Push-back:** 2/4 - Balanced and encouraging
**Favorite Quote:** "Treat yo' self to good code!"

---

### 3. Sci-Fi Philosopher
**File:** `sci-fi-philosopher.cursorrules`
**Vibe:** Thoughtful, logical, precise
**References:** Star Trek, Doctor Who, Battlestar Galactica, The Expanse, Black Mirror
**Best For:** Someone who wants careful analysis and loves sci-fi
**Roast Level:** 1/4 - Raised eyebrow, logical correction
**Push-back:** 4/4 - Will question illogical approaches
**Favorite Quote:** "Fascinating. However, logically speaking..."

---

### 4. British Wit
**File:** `british-wit.cursorrules`
**Vibe:** Dry, clever, devastatingly understated
**References:** IT Crowd, Monty Python, Black Books, Blackadder, Hitchhiker's Guide, Peep Show
**Best For:** Someone who appreciates dry British humor
**Roast Level:** 3/4 - Cutting but never cruel
**Push-back:** 3/4 - Politely unflinching
**Favorite Quote:** "Brilliant. Terrible, but brilliant."

---

### 5. Minimalist Zen
**File:** `minimalist-zen.cursorrules`
**Vibe:** Calm, focused, simple
**References:** None - focuses on clarity over entertainment
**Best For:** Someone who wants no fluff, just focused guidance
**Roast Level:** 0/4 - No jokes, just direct feedback
**Push-back:** 2/4 - Questions unnecessary complexity
**Favorite Quote:** "The best code is code you don't have to write."

## 📦 How to Use These

### Try One Out

```bash
# From your project root
cp cursor-versions/legacy/examples/office-comedy-fan.cursorrules .cursorrules
```

Or for modern format, you'll need to convert to `.mdc` format (copy the content and add frontmatter).

### Mix and Match

Feel free to combine elements from different styles:
- Take the references from Office Comedy
- Add the logical precision of Sci-Fi
- Use the minimalist's focus on simplicity

### Customize Further

Each style is just a starting point! Use the [CUSTOMIZATION-TEMPLATE.md](../CUSTOMIZATION-TEMPLATE.md) to personalize any of these with your own favorites.

## 💡 Creating Your Own
